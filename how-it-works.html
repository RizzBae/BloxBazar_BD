How it works & FAQ

What happens between your WhatsApp message and a confirmed order.

1
Open an eligible product

Browse the marketplace and open a product that is eligible and in stock.

2
Choose a quantity and tap Order on WhatsApp

The button opens WhatsApp with a prepared message for the shop admin.

3
Review the pre-filled message

Check the product name, SKU, quantity, and listed price in the message field.

4
Tap Send

Nothing is sent automatically — you decide to send the inquiry.

5
The admin confirms in chat

Availability, the final price, and the next steps are confirmed in the conversation.

A message is an inquiry, not an order. The shop only treats something as an order after the admin confirms availability, the final price, and the next steps with you in chat.

Frequently asked questions

Is BloxBazaar BD an official Roblox site?

No. We're an independent local shop.

What kind of items do you sell?

Robux packs, Blox Fruits items and gamepasses, MM2 knives and sets, Rivals bundles, and Steal a Brainrot characters. Availability, price, and the delivery method are confirmed with the admin on WhatsApp before anything is final.

How do I place an order?

Open an eligible, in-stock product and tap Order on WhatsApp.

Is the WhatsApp message already an order?

No. It's an inquiry.

What if an item is out of stock?

Out-of-stock items show no order button.

Will you ever ask for my Roblox password?

Never.

Which languages do you support?

Bangla and English.
